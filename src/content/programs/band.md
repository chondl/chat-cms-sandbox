Band is the largest program the Boosters support, and the one with the most
moving parts — literally. Students rehearse before school through the fall
competition season, then shift to concert repertoire after winter break.

## What parents do

Volunteers load and unload equipment, hem uniforms, staff the water table at
away games, and drive the trailer. No musical experience required; a willingness
to be outdoors in October is helpful.

## What Booster funds pay for

Sheet music, instrument repair, and the truck rental for competition weekends.
The school district funds the teaching staff — the Boosters support, the school
runs the program.
