# About the Parent Club

The Huron Visual and Performing Arts Parent Club is an independent,
all-volunteer organization supporting the visual and performing arts at Huron
High School.

We bring together parents, guardians, alumni, teachers, students, and community
members who believe that arts education should be ambitious, accessible, and a
little bit louder than strictly necessary.

There is no membership fee, no audition, and no minimum volunteer commitment.
You can lead a committee, help for twenty minutes at a concert, donate a box of
paintbrushes, or simply attend a meeting and find out what is happening.

> **The Parent Club supports. The school teaches and runs the programs.**

---

## Our mission

Our mission is to help every Huron student participate fully in the arts,
regardless of experience, background, or ability to pay.

We work to:

- Expand opportunities beyond the regular classroom budget
- Reduce financial barriers for students and families
- Support performances, exhibitions, festivals, and educational travel
- Provide practical volunteer help for teachers and student groups
- Build an enthusiastic audience for student artists
- Celebrate creative work in all its polished, unfinished, surprising, and
  occasionally papier-mâché-covered forms

## What volunteers do

Money helps, but many arts events depend just as much on people showing up.

Parent Club volunteers may:

- Staff concessions and ticket tables
- Move instruments, costumes, scenery, and display panels
- Drive rental trucks
- Assemble concert programs
- Photograph events
- Hem costumes and repair buttons
- Paint sets
- Hang student artwork
- Provide meals during long rehearsals
- Welcome visiting schools
- Help with fundraising events
- Return six rolls of unused silver duct tape to the correct cabinet

No special skill is required for most jobs. When a task requires particular
training — such as operating theatrical equipment or handling school funds — the
volunteer coordinator will make that clear.

Students are always supervised according to school and district policies.

## Student assistance

We do not want cost to prevent a student from participating.

Families may request confidential assistance with eligible program expenses such
as:

- Festival or competition fees
- Required performance clothing
- Art materials
- Instrument rentals
- Field trips
- Overnight travel
- Production participation costs

Requests are reviewed privately by the appropriate school staff member and the
Parent Club treasurer. Financial assistance decisions are never made by other
students or publicly discussed at Parent Club meetings.

For assistance, contact the student's teacher or program director.

---

## How decisions are made

Funding requests usually begin with a teacher or program director. Requests are
reviewed based on:

1. Educational value
2. Number of students served
3. Urgency
4. Availability of school or district funding
5. Available Parent Club funds
6. Whether anyone can explain why the department needs 400 tiny plastic frogs

Routine requests may be approved by the board. Larger or unusual expenditures
are discussed at an open membership meeting.

The Parent Club publishes an annual summary of income, expenses, major
purchases, and restricted program balances.

## Meetings

Board and general meetings are open to all families and community members.

- **When** — first Tuesday of each month during the school year
- **Time** — 7:00–8:15 p.m.
- **Where** — the Huron High School choir room, unless displaced by rehearsal,
  construction, standardized testing, or an unexplained quantity of risers

Meetings generally include:

- A short report from each program
- Financial updates
- Upcoming volunteer needs
- Funding requests
- Event planning
- One discussion that lasts seven minutes longer than expected

There is no requirement to speak, volunteer, or already know anyone. Visitors
are welcome to listen.

## Annual events

### Fall Arts Welcome Night

An informal evening for new and returning families to meet teachers, student
leaders, and Parent Club volunteers. Usually includes cookies, student
performances, and at least one parent discovering that "tech week" does not
refer to computer repair.

### Winter Showcase

A combined celebration featuring music, theater excerpts, dance, and student
artwork. The exact format changes each year depending on schedules, available
space, and whether the giant moon is still structurally sound.

### Pancakes & Paintbrushes

Our unnecessarily ambitious spring fundraiser includes breakfast, student
demonstrations, a silent auction, and supervised community mural painting.

### Senior Arts Celebration

A year-end reception recognizing graduating students who participated in Huron's
arts programs. Seniors are invited to share a favorite memory, future plan, or
object they found backstage and have been meaning to return.

contribution for an eligible program or approved campaign.

### Attend student events

A full audience matters. Applause is useful, affordable, and does not require

## Donations

The Parent Club accepts donations throughout the year.

Contributions may support:

- General arts programming
- Student financial assistance
- A specific eligible program
- A board-approved project or campaign

Restricted gifts are used for their designated purpose whenever possible. When a
project is fully funded or can no longer proceed, the board may contact the
donor or redirect the funds to a closely related need consistent with
organizational policy.

Donation acknowledgments are provided for tax purposes when applicable.

**The Huron Visual and Performing Arts Parent Club is a fictional organization
created for demonstration purposes. Please do not mail it a real check.**

## Reimbursements

Volunteers and staff should obtain approval before making purchases on behalf of
the Parent Club.

To request reimbursement:

1. Complete the reimbursement form.
2. Attach an itemized receipt.
3. Identify the program and approved purpose.
4. Submit the request within 30 days.
5. Do not leave the only copy of the receipt in the pocket of clothing being
   used as a theater costume.

Sales tax should not be paid when the organization's exemption can be used.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to become a member?

No formal enrollment is required. If you are part of the Huron arts community,
you are welcome.

### Is there a membership fee?

No.

### Do I need arts experience?

No. Some of our most valuable volunteers have never played an instrument,
appeared onstage, mixed paint, or correctly folded a fitted sheet.

### Can middle-school families participate?

Yes. Future Huron families are welcome at public events and general meetings.

### Can students attend Parent Club meetings?

Yes, unless a meeting includes a confidential personnel, financial-assistance,
or student matter.

### Can I volunteer during the school day?

Some roles are available during the day. School visitor and volunteer
requirements apply.

### Can the Parent Club influence casting or ensemble placement?

No. Instructional and artistic decisions belong to school staff.

### Are donations shared equally among programs?

Not necessarily. Spending reflects approved requests, restricted donations,
available funds, and current program needs. The board works to support all
disciplines fairly over time rather than dividing every dollar according to a
fixed formula.

### Who owns the equipment the Parent Club purchases?

Most equipment purchased for instructional use is donated to or held for the
benefit of the school program, subject to applicable policies and purchase
agreements.

### Where did the giant moon come from?

Minutes from the relevant meeting are inconclusive.

## Our guiding principles

We try to operate according to a few simple ideas:

- Students come first.
- Teachers direct the programs.
- Participation should be financially accessible.
- Volunteers deserve clear jobs and reasonable expectations.
- Financial decisions should be transparent.
- Every arts discipline deserves respect.
- A high school production does not need to look professional to be meaningful.
- It is possible to have too much glitter, but no one agrees on the exact
  amount.
