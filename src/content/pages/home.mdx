<Hero eyebrow="Curtain up on the spring season" title="Every student on that stage">
The Huron Visual and Performing Arts Parent Club is the parent volunteer group
behind band, choir, theater, and orchestra at Huron High School. We raise the
money, drive the trailer, and hem the uniforms. The school runs the programs; we
make them possible.
</Hero>

<Reasons title="Why the Parent Club exists">
- Program budgets cover teachers and classrooms, not sheet music, bus rentals, or
  a lighting package for the spring musical.
- Every family should be able to say yes to a festival trip, so we quietly cover
  fees for students who need it.
- Four programs sharing one parent club means one fundraiser instead of four,
  and one ask of every family instead of four.
</Reasons>

## This season

Rehearsals run all year, and there is a performance almost every month. The
[season calendar](/season/) has the dates; the [programs page](/programs/) has
the details.
