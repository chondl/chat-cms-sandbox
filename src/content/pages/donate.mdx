## Ways to give

Checks are made out to **Sandbox Arts Boosters Foundation** (tax ID 00-0000000,
a fixture number that is not a real EIN) and can be dropped at the school office
or handed to any board member.

Online gifts go through the giving page linked from the school website. Employer
matching forms should also use the legal name above.

<Reasons title="What your gift pays for">
- Sheet music, scripts, and performance licensing.
- Instrument repair, bow rehairing, and string replacement.
- Festival entry fees — including for students whose families cannot cover them.
- The truck rental that gets a marching band's equipment to a competition.
</Reasons>

## Questions

Robin Fixture handles Booster finances and can answer questions about receipts,
matching gifts, and what a donation is earmarked for. Catch her at a board
meeting or leave a note in the office.
